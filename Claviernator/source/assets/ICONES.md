# Icônes de Fortinatus

Créées le 16 octobre 2026 avec l’outil imagegen intégré, fond transparent.

- `logo-clavier-v1.png` : logo clavier original, utilisé dans la fenêtre, la barre des tâches et l’icône près de l’horloge.
- `ajouter-touche-v1.png` : touche « + », utilisée par le bouton Ajouter une touche.
- `fortinatus.png` et `fortinatus.ico` : déclinaisons Windows du logo, obtenues par réduction et conversion avec `scripts/make_icon.py`. La transparence est conservée.

## Prompt du logo

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final application icon for Fortinatus, an accessible Windows keyboard remapping app. Create ONE beautiful polished modern keyboard logo, no letters and no words. A centered distinctive simplified keyboard symbol in pale luminous mint on a deep petrol turquoise rounded square app tile, softly beveled edges with a restrained elegant turquoise gradient, confident crisp geometry, tasteful premium product identity. The keyboard is the hero: large horizontal rounded body, three neatly aligned rows of chunky simple rounded key shapes and one clearly recognizable long space bar, straight front view, perfectly balanced symmetrical composition, generous but small padding, instantly recognizable at 32 pixels. The square tile fills about 90 percent of the square canvas; rounded corners outside the tile must be genuinely transparent. Turquoise palette #0b292d, #15565b, #4fbdba, #b3f5e5. Clean bold silhouette, high contrast and minimal detail, sharp professional icon finish. Avoid text, F, initials, arrows, mouse, accessibility person symbols, neon flares, busy embellishments, tiny key legends, photorealistic hardware, perspective distortion, mockups, watermarks. Square 1024x1024 composition.

## Prompt de la touche d’ajout

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: a single small transparent PNG button icon for the Fortinatus turquoise Windows app, used next to the label 'Ajouter une touche'. Draw ONE elegant bold rounded keyboard keycap with a centered plus sign. No keyboard array, just one key with a clear + sign. Front view with very slight raised thickness at the bottom edge, sophisticated modern polished geometry, deep petrol teal key face (#164c50), subtle teal highlight around a softly rounded square edge, pale mint thick plus (#c3ffec). Minimal confident silhouette, large plus, no tiny detail, readable when reduced to 24x24 pixels. Key fills roughly 88 percent of square canvas. All background outside the key is genuinely transparent, no ambient shadow on the canvas, no background square badge, no text or letters except the single plus symbol. Square format. Final production icon asset, no mockup, no watermark.
